# Real-photo background fixture

`fronalpstock-3840.jpg` is the 3840 × 1725 Wikimedia thumbnail of **Fronalpstock big.jpg**,
photographed by **Hannes Röst**. Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fronalpstock_big.jpg>.
Downloaded 2026-10-20 from <https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3f/Fronalpstock_big.jpg/3840px-Fronalpstock_big.jpg>.

The photograph is licensed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>.
This fixture is the source's resized thumbnail; no further edits were made. Background tests
resize it and composite the app over it. This attribution and license apply to the photograph
and its image derivatives; it is test-only, not a bundled/default product background.

Use this high-detail real landscape for import/performance/visual acceptance instead of a
synthetic gradient. Tiny synthetic codec fixtures remain useful for strict validation tests.

`dynamic-wallpaper.mp4` and `dynamic-wallpaper.webm` are two-second synthetic test videos
generated locally with FFmpeg's `testsrc` pattern on 2026-10-23. They are test-only media
used to verify local video wallpaper decode, preview, playback and recovery; they are not
bundled/default product wallpapers.

The separate local visual acceptance report also uses **Lake mapourika NZ.jpeg** by
**Richard Palmer**, from <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lake_mapourika_NZ.jpeg>,
under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license. Its local screenshots composite the app over the
photo; the lake image is not committed or bundled. Both photographs' screenshot/video
derivatives retain the photograph attribution and CC BY-SA 3.0 license; these terms do
not change the application source-code license. The user-supplied mountain photo is
kept only in local acceptance evidence.
